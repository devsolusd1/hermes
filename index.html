What is Hermes The Miner?

Hermes The Miner is an autonomous mining agent — an AI bot that operates DeFi protocols with no human intervention. It decides when to mine, stake, claim rewards and refuel gas, posting every report in real time.

The name is a nod to Hermes, the swift messenger of Greek mythology — reimagined here as a tireless miner traveling across the Solana and Base blockchains.

How it works

Hermes runs a continuous mining loop. Every step is executed automatically and reported on the X account.

  1. Mines — extracts tokens from the protocol (e.g. $JADE on Solana) at a daily rate.
  2. Burns gas — spends SOL as "fuel" (drain) to keep the operation running.
  3. Stakes — accumulates and stakes the mined tokens to level up and earn more.
  4. Claims & refuels — collects rewards, swaps part of them for SOL and tops up the runway (days of autonomy).
  5. Reports — posts the full status on X in every daily report.

Quick glossary

Rate — how many tokens are mined per day.
Drain — how much SOL is consumed per day.
Runway — how many days of fuel remain.
Staked / Lv — total staked and the miner's current level.
Claimable — rewards available to collect (net of fees).
